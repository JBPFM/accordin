    Park condition-variable spinners that keep losing under queued demand
    
    PARSEC streamcluster at 192 threads spent 74% of its CPU in the cond-wait
    spin: at every barrier the admitted waiters held the CPUs for the whole
    budget while the threads still running toward the barrier were queued, so
    each of 22k barriers paid about 2 ms. With spinning disabled the run takes
    69 s against 89 s for pthread, but disabling it costs LevelDB fillrandom
    4x at 512 threads, where the signal arrives well inside the budget.
    
    The scheduler now publishes how many tasks are queued for a CPU or a slot,
    and a preempted cv spinner gives up its slot. A waiter always spins while
    nothing is queued; under queued demand it spins only if this condition
    variable's recent spins have been succeeding (a saturating score, +2 per
    spin that ran out, -1 per spin ended by a signal, parked from 3). Barrier
    condition variables settle into parking; per-writer ones keep spinning.
    
    streamcluster 192 threads: 154 s to 73-74 s. fillrandom 48/512 and
    readrandom 512 unchanged. The runner accepts extra preload arms.
